# Clinical rules

The normative source is [clinical-spec/clinical-rules.json](../clinical-spec/clinical-rules.json), explained in [RULES.md](../clinical-spec/RULES.md). Source evidence and unresolved access limitations are in [SOURCES.md](../clinical-spec/SOURCES.md). This page intentionally does not maintain another threshold table.

SIC threshold flags are numerical only and require sepsis for clinical interpretation. DIC threshold flags require a compatible underlying disorder and do not establish a diagnosis. Low scores do not rule out coagulopathy/DIC. Serial evaluation remains necessary when suspicion persists.
