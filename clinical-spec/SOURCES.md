## Important unresolved verification

The 2025 DIC change (>3 and >7 ULN) is supported by the accessible primary-text transcription. Platelet/PT/fibrinogen boundaries and total threshold are provisionally implemented per the requested contract and unchanged historical criteria, but the publisher Table 1 has not been directly inspected. Do not label this ruleset independently validated. A reviewer must compare all endpoints against an authorized full copy and record exact page/table and disposition.

SOFA tables report ranges at laboratory precision and rounded SI equivalents. We deliberately document a continuous conventional-unit policy rather than silently claiming exact agreement at every printed boundary. Approval of that policy is a release blocker. Pharmacologic support must reflect the defined SOFA assessment period, not an arbitrary momentary dose. No automatically inferred drug equivalence is used.

The selected product target is ISTH 2025 overt DIC plus established SIC. A literature search also surfaced newer proposed scores; these are not silently substituted. Recheck corrections/errata and source status during release review.
